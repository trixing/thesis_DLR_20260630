\paragraph{Use of AI tools.}
During the preparation of this thesis (2024--2026), AI tools were utilized: the AI assistant Claude (Anthropic), including the coding assistant Claude Code, was used under the author's direction as a programming and writing aid. Its use comprised:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Code:} Implementation and modification of MATLAB code in the author's GitHub repositories, documented as pull requests in these repositories,
    \begin{itemize}
        \item for the wind turbine controller studies of Chapter~\ref{sec:Application to Wind Turbine Control4}: the design of filters and additional states of the predictive controller to improve its performance, the retuning of the controller gains, the combination of the predictive controller with the individual pitch controller, and the re-evaluation of the newly designed and optimized controllers with the criteria defined in this thesis;
        \item for the wind farm controller studies of Chapter~\ref{sec:ApplicationtoWindFarmControl}: the redesign of the system identification signals; the estimation of the effective wind speeds from the turbine powers, which makes the closed-loop operation independent of wind speed measurements; the redesign of the sensitivity study of the parameters of the adaptive farm controller; and the redesign of the nonlinear features of the combined thrust and yaw farm controller as functions of the filtered control signals, which are included as model states, together with the corresponding adaptation of the MPC prediction;
        \item for the MATLAB scripts that generate the figures and tables of this thesis directly from the simulation data.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Text:} Review of the chapters for consistency among text, equations, tables, and code; proofreading and language editing; and suggestions for passages, captions, and revised versions of sections of Chapters~1 to~6.
\end{itemize}
In addition, the author used ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity for language editing, support in generating and revising the MATLAB scripts used to produce the figures and tables, and literature searches. All results obtained with AI tools were carefully reviewed and verified by the author. The models listed above were proposed or refined with the help of AI tools; their evaluation and selection, as well as the design decisions and conclusions, are the author's. Suggested text was reviewed, revised, and integrated by the author, who is responsible for the entire content of this thesis.
